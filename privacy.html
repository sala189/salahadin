My Zikr

Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

My Zikr is a free Islamic app. We built it so it works without collecting information about you.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or contacts. We do not show ads and we do not sell or share any data.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to us or stored on any server. You can turn location off at any time and choose a city instead.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules azan and reminder alerts on your phone. No notification data leaves your device.

Settings and progress

Your settings, bookmarks and azkar progress are saved only on your phone. Deleting the app removes them.

Content from Quran Foundation

To show Quran text, mushaf pages and recitations, the app downloads content from Quran Foundation services. These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the content, such as the page or ayah number, and the technical details every internet request includes, such as your IP address. See the Quran Foundation privacy policy for how they handle this.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
