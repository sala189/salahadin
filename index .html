My Zikr

Free · No ads · iOS and Android

Prayer times, Quran and daily azkar in one simple app

My Zikr helps Muslims keep their day with Allah. It is free, has no ads and needs no account. It works in English, Arabic, Amharic and Afaan Oromoo.

  • Prayer times from your location or a chosen city, with the main calculation methods and the Hanafi or standard Asr setting.
  • Azan notifications with a choice of muezzin voices and a reminder before each prayer.
  • Qibla compass and Hijri calendar.
  • Daily azkar for morning, evening, after prayer and before sleep, from Hisn al-Muslim, with a counter and recitation.
  • The Holy Quran on the Madinah mushaf pages, with recitation by well-known qaris.
